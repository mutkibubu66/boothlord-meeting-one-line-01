## The line

> We're pushing the launch by two weeks because nobody in this meeting has the authority to approve anything, including this meeting.

One sentence that replaces the whole meeting: the decision (launch delayed two weeks), the reason (nobody present can approve anything), and the twist — the meeting itself is one of the things nobody here can approve. Anyone who has sat through a pointless meeting recognises it instantly.

python3 main.py
